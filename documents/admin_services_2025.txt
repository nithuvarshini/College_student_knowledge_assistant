GREENFIELD UNIVERSITY - ADMINISTRATIVE SERVICES GUIDE 2025
Issued by: Registrar's Office | Updated: July 2025

SECTION 1: BONAFIDE CERTIFICATE

1.1 Purpose
A Bonafide Certificate confirms that a student is currently enrolled at Greenfield University. It is commonly required for bank loans passport applications visa processing government scholarship portals and bus or railway concession passes.

1.2 Application Procedure
Step 1: Log in to the Student Portal at portal.greenfield.edu
Step 2: Navigate to Services then Certificates then Bonafide Certificate
Step 3: Fill in the purpose of the certificate and submit
Step 4: Collect the signed certificate from the Registrar's Office Block A Room 102

Processing Time: 3 working days from the date of application.
Fee: Free of cost for the first copy. Rs. 50 per copy for additional copies.

1.3 Urgent Processing
For urgent requirements such as visa court or emergency students may apply in person with supporting documents. Urgent certificates are issued within 24 working hours subject to officer availability.

SECTION 2: TRANSCRIPT AND MARK SHEETS

2.1 Official Transcript
Official transcripts are issued by the Examination Cell. Request via the student portal under Services then Transcripts.
Processing Time: 7 working days.
Fee: Rs. 200 per copy and Rs. 500 for sealed attested copy for foreign universities.

2.2 Duplicate Mark Sheet
For lost or damaged semester mark sheets submit a written application to the Examination Cell and attach an FIR copy for lost documents or the damaged original.
Fee: Rs. 300 per mark sheet.
Processing Time: 10 working days.

SECTION 3: ID CARD AND LIBRARY CARD

3.1 Duplicate ID Card
Step 1: Report loss to the Security Office Block G
Step 2: Submit Form ID-DUP-01 at the Student Affairs Office
Step 3: Pay Rs. 200 at the Finance Office and attach receipt
Processing Time: 3 working days.

3.2 Duplicate Library Card
Apply at the Library Help Desk with Rs. 100 fee and ID proof.

SECTION 4: MIGRATION CERTIFICATE AND TRANSFER CERTIFICATE

Migration Certificate and Transfer Certificate are issued only upon withdrawal or completion of the programme.
Application: Student Affairs Office Block A Room 101.
Processing Time: 15 working days after clearance from all departments.
Clearance required from Library Finance Hostel Labs and T&P Cell.

SECTION 5: NAME AND DATE OF BIRTH CORRECTION

Name or date of birth corrections in university records require original 10th Board Mark Sheet as proof and an Affidavit from a Notary Public and an application to the Registrar's Office.
Processing Time: 21 working days as it involves academic council approval.

SECTION 6: HOSTEL AND ACCOMMODATION

6.1 Hostel Availability
Boys Hostel is in Blocks H1 and H2 with capacity for 600 students.
Girls Hostel is in Blocks H3 and H4 with capacity for 500 students.
Hostel allocation is on first-come first-served basis for outstation students.

6.2 Hostel Fee Per Semester
3-sharing Room : Rs. 18000
Double Sharing : Rs. 22000
Single Room    : Rs. 30000
All fees include mess charges for 3 meals per day.

6.3 Hostel Rules
Curfew is 10:00 PM on weekdays and 11:00 PM on weekends.
No visitors of opposite gender inside hostel blocks.
Alcohol smoking and illegal substances are strictly prohibited.
