GREENFIELD UNIVERSITY - FEES SCHOLARSHIPS AND FINANCIAL AID 2025-26
Issued by: Finance and Scholarships Office | Date: June 2025

SECTION 1: FEE STRUCTURE

1.1 Tuition Fees Per Semester
B.Tech All Branches  : Rs. 55000 per semester
BCA and BBA          : Rs. 35000 per semester
M.Tech               : Rs. 65000 per semester
MBA                  : Rs. 70000 per semester

1.2 Other Fees Annual collected in Odd Semester
Examination Fee      : Rs. 2500
Library Fee          : Rs. 1000
Lab Maintenance Fee  : Rs. 3000
Student Activity Fund: Rs. 500
Identity Card Fee    : Rs. 200 for first year only

1.3 Fee Payment Deadline
Odd Semester Fee Due : 31 July 2025
Even Semester Fee Due: 15 January 2026

1.4 Late Fee Rule
Students who fail to pay fees by the due date will be charged a late fee as follows.
1 to 7 days late   : Rs. 500 additional
8 to 15 days late  : Rs. 1500 additional
Beyond 15 days     : Rs. 3000 additional and name removed from class roll

Students removed from roll must apply for re-admission through the office subject to management approval.

SECTION 2: SCHOLARSHIPS

2.1 Merit Scholarship for All Years
Eligibility requires CGPA of 8.5 or above in the previous semester with no active backlogs and minimum 85% attendance.
Benefit is 25% waiver on tuition fee for the next semester.
Application is automatic based on results and no separate application is needed.

2.2 Government Scholarships
The following scholarships are facilitated by the university.
Post-Matric Scholarship for SC and ST students provides full tuition fee reimbursement.
OBC Scholarship provides up to Rs. 20000 per year.
Minority Scholarship Central provides up to Rs. 30000 per year.
EWS Scholarship provides 10% fee concession.
Application deadline is within 60 days of semester commencement.

2.3 Sports Scholarship
Students who represent the university or state in recognized sports may apply for a sports scholarship of up to Rs. 15000 per year.

2.4 Need-Based Financial Aid
Students from families with annual income below Rs. 250000 may apply for need-based aid covering up to 50% of tuition fees.
Supporting documents required are income certificate from Tahsildar and ration card and bank statement of last 6 months.

2.5 Scholarship for 2nd Year Students
Second-year students who scored above 80% in their 1st-year aggregate are eligible for the Star Performer Award which is a one-time cash prize of Rs. 5000 and a merit certificate.

SECTION 3: REFUND POLICY
Withdrawal before semester start: 100% refund of tuition with Rs. 500 processing fee retained.
Withdrawal within first 10 days of semester: 75% refund.
Withdrawal after 10 days: No refund of tuition fee.
Examination and other fees are non-refundable.
