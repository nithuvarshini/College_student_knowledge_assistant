GREENFIELD UNIVERSITY - PLACEMENT AND INTERNSHIP POLICY 2025
Issued by: Training and Placement Cell | Effective: August 2025

SECTION 1: PLACEMENT ELIGIBILITY

1.1 Academic Criteria
To be eligible for campus placement drives a student must satisfy ALL of the following conditions.
Minimum CGPA of 6.0 on a 10-point scale at the time of registration.
No active backlog subjects at the time of the placement drive.
Minimum 75% aggregate attendance throughout the programme.

1.2 Backlog Policy
Students with ONE cleared backlog meaning history of arrear but passed may be considered eligible subject to individual company policy. Some companies enforce a strict no-backlog-history policy and students will be informed accordingly.
Students with active uncleared backlogs are NOT eligible to participate in any placement drive until the backlog is cleared.

1.3 Registration
All eligible students must register with the Training and Placement Cell by filling Form TP-REG-01 available at the T&P Office Block D Ground Floor. Late registration is not accepted.

SECTION 2: PLACEMENT PROCESS

2.1 Drive Stages
A typical placement drive consists of the following stages.
Stage 1: Online Aptitude Test
Stage 2: Group Discussion (not conducted by all companies)
Stage 3: Technical Interview (one or more rounds)
Stage 4: HR Interview

2.2 Multiple Offers
A student who has accepted one job offer may not participate in subsequent drives unless the offer is withdrawn by the company. This policy ensures fairness for unplaced students.

2.3 Dream Company Policy
Students are allowed to participate in up to 2 dream company drives even after receiving an offer if those companies are pre-listed by the T&P Cell at the start of the academic year.

SECTION 3: INTERNSHIP GUIDELINES

3.1 Mandatory Internship
All students in the 3rd year must complete a minimum 4-week internship during the summer vacation. This is a programme requirement.

3.2 Documents Required for Internship Approval
Students must submit the following to the Department Internship Coordinator.
Internship Offer Letter from the company on company letterhead.
Student Internship Application Form which is Form INT-01.
Parent or Guardian consent letter.
Updated resume.
Faculty mentor approval signature.

3.3 Internship Report
After completing the internship students must submit an Internship Completion Report within 15 days of rejoining college. Report must include a company certificate daily log and a project summary of minimum 20 pages.

3.4 Credit Allocation
Successful internship completion carries 2 credits toward the degree. Failure to complete or submit report results in 0 credits and a grade of F.
