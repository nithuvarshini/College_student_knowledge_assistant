GREENFIELD UNIVERSITY - STUDENT HANDBOOK 2025
Version: 2025 | Approved by: Academic Council | Date: January 2025

CHAPTER 1: ATTENDANCE POLICY

1.1 Minimum Attendance Requirement
Students must maintain a minimum of 75% attendance in each subject to be eligible to appear for semester end examinations. This is a mandatory requirement under University regulation UG-ATD-01.

1.2 Shortage of Attendance
Students with attendance between 65% and 74% may apply for a medical condonation with valid medical certificates approved by the University Medical Officer. Applications must be submitted within 7 days of the last working day of the semester.
Students with attendance below 65% will be detained from appearing in the semester examination and must repeat the semester.

1.3 Attendance Rounding
Attendance is not rounded up. A student with 74.9% attendance is considered to have a shortage.

CHAPTER 2: EXAMINATION RULES

2.1 Internal Assessment
Three internal assessments are conducted each semester. Best two out of three scores are considered. Each IA carries 30 marks scaled to 20 marks for final grading.

2.2 Re-test Policy
A student who misses an internal assessment due to a medical emergency may apply for a re-test by submitting Form IA-RT-02 to the department office within 5 working days of the missed test. Re-tests are conducted at the discretion of the Head of Department.

2.3 Malpractice
Any student found guilty of malpractice during examinations will be suspended for one semester and the subject will be graded as F (Fail). Repeat offenders face permanent expulsion.

CHAPTER 3: GRADING SYSTEM

3.1 Grade Points
O  (Outstanding)   : 91-100 marks = 10 grade points
A+ (Excellent)     : 81-90  marks = 9 grade points
A  (Very Good)     : 71-80  marks = 8 grade points
B+ (Good)          : 61-70  marks = 7 grade points
B  (Above Average) : 51-60  marks = 6 grade points
C  (Average)       : 45-50  marks = 5 grade points
P  (Pass)          : 40-44  marks = 4 grade points
F  (Fail)          : Below 40     = 0 grade points

3.2 CGPA Calculation
CGPA = Sum of (Grade Points x Credits) divided by Total Credits attempted.

CHAPTER 4: DISCIPLINARY RULES

4.1 Code of Conduct
Students are expected to behave with decorum on campus. Ragging, harassment, and substance abuse are strictly prohibited and punishable by expulsion.

4.2 Dress Code
Smart casuals are permitted Monday to Thursday. No sleeveless or shorts inside academic buildings.

CHAPTER 5: GRIEVANCE REDRESSAL

5.1 Student Grievance Cell
Students may submit grievances online at grievance.greenfield.edu or in person at the Student Affairs Office, Block A, Room 101. Response is guaranteed within 10 working days.

5.2 Anti-Ragging Committee
