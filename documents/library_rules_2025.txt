GREENFIELD UNIVERSITY - LIBRARY RULES AND REGULATIONS 2025
Issued by: University Librarian | Effective: July 2025

SECTION 1: MEMBERSHIP AND ACCESS

1.1 Eligibility
All enrolled students faculty and staff are automatic members of the university library. Library card is issued along with the identity card during admission.

1.2 Library Hours
Monday to Friday : 8:00 AM to 9:00 PM
Saturday         : 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM
Sunday           : Closed but open during exam season from 10 AM to 4 PM

SECTION 2: BORROWING RULES

2.1 Book Loan Limits
UG Students   : 3 books at a time for 14 days
PG Students   : 5 books at a time for 21 days
Faculty Staff : 10 books at a time for 30 days

2.2 Renewals
Books may be renewed twice online via library.greenfield.edu or in person provided no other student has reserved the book.

2.3 Reservation
Students may reserve a book currently on loan. When available the student will be notified via email. Reserved books must be collected within 2 working days or the reservation is cancelled.

SECTION 3: FINE STRUCTURE FOR LATE RETURNS

3.1 Overdue Fine
UG Students   : Rs. 2 per book per day after due date
PG Students   : Rs. 3 per book per day after due date
Faculty Staff : Rs. 5 per book per day after due date

3.2 Maximum Fine
The maximum fine per book is capped at Rs. 200. After reaching maximum fine the book is considered lost and the student must pay replacement cost.

3.3 Lost or Damaged Books
If a borrowed book is lost or damaged beyond use the student must pay the current market price of the book plus a processing fee of Rs. 100.

3.4 Fine Clearance
All library fines must be cleared before appearing in semester end examinations and before collecting hall tickets and before applying for bonafide certificates or transcripts and before graduation or convocation.

SECTION 4: READING ROOM RULES
Maintain strict silence in the reading room.
Mobile phones must be on silent mode and calls are strictly prohibited inside.
Food and beverages are not allowed inside the library.
Bags must be deposited at the bag counter before entering.

SECTION 5: DIGITAL LIBRARY
Students have access to NPTEL Video Lectures and DELNET inter-library loan network and ShodhGanga thesis database and IEEE Xplore for engineering students and JSTOR for arts and management.
Login credentials are provided during orientation.
